# Chronobound

## Storyline

Once upon a time in Victorian London, there was a giant clock that keep track of the lifespan of every human being controlled by the government, from birth to death. One day, people discovered that they could steal other people's time by killing them, allowing themselves to live longer. This discovery led to a massive killing. Hundreds and thousands of people died, and eventually, monsters began to appear across the city. You are just an ordinary human trying to survive in this chaotic world. With your own time running low, you decide to hunt and kill monsters to steal their time and extend your life.

In this game, when your remaining time gets low, you will enter a strange world called Limbo, where everything starts to look weird and spooky. When your time finally runs out, you will enter Hell, a world where everything is burning. Fight your way through the different layers of this world, kill monsters and reach the final boss. But when you finally defeat it, you discover that all the monsters you have been killing were actually humans. 

After defeating the final boss, the giant clock(government) chooses you to become its servant. You continue hunting humans and monsters to collect time for the clock. However, as you consume more corrupted time perhaps, you are becoming one of the monsters too...
